# Rogue Circuit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Rogue Circuit does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data.

The game does not require an account and does not include advertising,
analytics, tracking, or online services. Game progress, settings, and player
preferences are stored locally on the device. This local data is not sent to
the developer and can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling the
game.

The store or operating system used to download and run Rogue Circuit may
process information independently under its own privacy policy. That platform
processing is not controlled by Rogue Circuit.

Rogue Circuit does not knowingly collect personal information from children or
other users.
